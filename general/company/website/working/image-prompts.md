## Quick guidance on generation
- Generate at **3:2 landscape** for the main hero/pillar slots (1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 9, 10).
- Generate at **4:3** for the small Helios overview tiles (5, 6).
- If a result feels "too gold" or "too lit," dial back with: *"reduce gold accent intensity by 40%, lean into deep navy ambient"*.
- If the architecture feels generic, add: *"contemporary Filipino architectural sensibility — louvered timber screens, sculptural concrete forms, tropical material restraint."*
- Always reject takes with: human faces front-on, readable text/logos, garish saturation, mismatched palette (warm reds/oranges other than the single brand-red signage moment).
