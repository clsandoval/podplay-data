PoE-to-USB-C adapter, 1 per iPad / court. Powers the iPad directly from a PoE switch port **and** passes data over USB-C — the iPad enrolls and gets its DHCP lease through it. Must do power *and* data; a power-only splitter is not a substitute.

Ubiquiti UACC-Adapter-PoE-USBC (passive, 10W, GbE data) — what Tela Park bought at ₱1,332. The **catalog default is the generic PH unit** ([[poe-usb-c-adapter-generic]], ~₱236, FE data); this is the higher-spec alternate. POE Texas AF-USBC-PD (US import) also works.

**Do not** use the 25W UACC-PoE+-USBC on dense venues — at 14 courts it pushes the 48-port switch to its PoE ceiling.
