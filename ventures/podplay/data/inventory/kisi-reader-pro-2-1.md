**The 2.1, not the Pro 2:** adds Apple ECP 2.0 + offline support, and the PH Kisi supplier doesn't stock the older generation anyway.

**Port placement (Kosmas deviation):** goes on the **UDM-SE's PoE ports first** (normally 6 free), overflowing to the switch only when the gateway runs out. PodPlay's guides put every reader on the switch — an installer following them verbatim won't do this; call it out per venue. Tag each UDM port carrying a reader onto the ACCESS CONTROL VLAN.
