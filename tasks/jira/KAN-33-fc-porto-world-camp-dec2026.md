# KAN-33: FC Porto World Camp Dec2026

## Description

FC Porto Dragon Force World Camp is an international football training camp program created by FC Porto through its Dragon Force youth development system. It brings FC Porto’s academy methodology to different countries, allowing young players — usually ages 6 to 16 — to train directly under certified FC Porto coaches.

### JC Golez — 2026-06-09

The discussion during the meeting today, June 9, 2026, focused on finalizing the logistics for the FC Porto football camp in the Philippines, tentatively scheduled for December 2026. 

- Key stakeholders coordinated on scheduling, curriculum enhancements, and commercial terms to ensure the event serves as a precursor to a permanent football school.

Operational Strategy & Scheduling

• Timeline: The target window is the second to third week of December. The team is currently verifying Christmas break schedules with local schools to avoid overlap with the resumption of classes in early January.

We can also do evening sessions if school is still ongoing.

• Regional Expansion: The plan includes a "Manila leg" and a shorter "Bacolod leg" (approximately three days) to increase brand exposure. Jorge confirmed that Porto can accommodate multiple locations and facilities.

• Porto Coach Logistics: To respect Portuguese and Filipino traditions, all coaches must return to Portugal by December 23. JC confirmed the schedule will allow them to be back in Portugal by December 21 or 22 or even earlier.

Curriculum & Value-Add Programs

The group discussed differentiating the camp from competitors (e.g., Barcelona, Villarreal) through specialized sessions:
• Nutrition & Psychology: Porto will provide "pedagogical activities," including nutrition and sports psychology. JC proposed involving parents in nutrition sessions to increase marketability which Jorge confirmed that it can be done by virtual sessions. Around 30mis-1hr

• Language Integration: The camp will include a basic Portuguese football vocabulary component (e.g., learning terms like passar for pass and cruzar for cross).

• Goalkeeper Training: While Porto’s methodology integrates goalkeepers into team play, they agreed to include 30–60 minutes of position-specific training daily.

• Performance Reports: Every participant will receive a performance report and certificate. Porto uses an online platform or PDF format for these evaluations, which will be delivered shortly after the camp concludes.

Commercials & Talent Pathway

• Cost Structure: The current contract specifies €3,600 for two coaches for a five-day camp. The team requested a revised proposal to include the additional three-day provincial leg and a third coach once schedule is finalized.

• Talent Identification: Jorge clarified that while "trials" are legally sensitive due to FIFA regulations, exceptional talent (18+) can be conducted in a way that there will be initial trials in Portugal, if the player passes, they return to their respective academy and continue training. Then performance evaluations will continue and if player progresses, they will be sent back for a month training then so on and so forth until they reach 18yrs old when Porto can sign them for a contract. 

On the other hand, player experiences can be given to top camp players to be sent to Portugal at the cost of KAVC.

A formal scouting report will be generated for all Camp participants.

• Long-term Goal: Both parties emphasized that the camp is a "loss leader" or brand-building exercise intended to lead to the establishment of a permanent FC Porto football school in the Philippines.

Next Steps

- Survey: KAVC will launch a survey this month to gauge interest and confirm the viability of the December dates.
- Final Proposal: Jorge will send a revised contract once Richard confirms the final dates and the requirement for three coaches.
School Calendars: The local team must finalize the school holiday list to lock the event dates.
