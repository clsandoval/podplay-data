# KAN-16: FC Porto Academy

## Description

FC Porto Academy is FC Porto’s youth program. It helps young players grow with a European approach: strong technique, smart tactics, solid fitness, and good character.

## Comments

### Richard Bachmann — 2026-05-11

From my understanding, the current proposal is to create a NEW company structure where we would hold 70% ownership while FA Manila retains 30%. Conceptually, I understand the value of using an existing club platform given the new NCR league structure and the difficulty of building a club from scratch today. However, the actual mechanics and structure of the deal are still not fully clear to us yet.

Before we move forward further, I think we need to get alignment and clarity on several areas:

1. Ownership & Deal Structure

- Is this essentially a share swap structure where the existing FA Manila stakeholders receive 30% ownership in the NEW company without a direct monetary buyout?
- Who exactly will own the remaining 30% on the FA Manila side?
- What exactly are we acquiring or gaining aside from the existing player/member base?   - League participation rights?   - Existing brand equity?   - Operational infrastructure?   - Coaching system?   - Existing sponsor relationships?
- Will the existing FA Manila entity continue operating separately, or will everything transition into the NEW company structure?

1. Shareholders & Corporate Information
We also need a clearer understanding of the current ownership and legal structure of FA Manila.

- Mico mentioned Sergie and two other parents currently own FA Manila. Are there additional shareholders beyond those mentioned?
- Since FA Manila is SEC registered, we should probably request the following as part of due diligence:   - Latest GIS   - Latest AFS   - Cap table / ownership structure   - Banking information   - Existing liabilities or obligations if any

1. Operations & Personnel
We should also clarify operational expectations early.

- Are we expected to retain:   - Existing coaching staff?   - The current Spanish Technical Director?   - Existing admin personnel?
- Are any of these contractual obligations or simply preferred arrangements?
- I also think it would be good for us to interview the admin team and selected coaches to better understand how the club currently operates.

1. League & Transition Questions
Need clarity as well on how the transition works operationally with the league.

- If the club eventually rebrands into Dragonforce FA Manila during an active season, will this create any league issues or restrictions?
- For players who become interested in transferring after participating in activities like the World Camp, my understanding is they would simply need to wait until the season ends before formally changing clubs. We should confirm this.

1. Membership Retention & Business Risk
This is probably one of the biggest business risks.
A large part of the value here is tied to the existing membership base. If after the transition a meaningful percentage of players leave, we need to understand:

- How that risk is viewed by both sides
- Whether there are retention strategies already in place
- How operational and financial responsibilities would be handled during the 2-3 year build period being proposed

Overall, I do agree there is strategic value in acquiring an existing operational platform instead of starting from zero, especially under the evolving NCR system. But before committing capital and resources, I think we need full clarity on:

- what exactly we are acquiring,
- what obligations come with it,
- what operational flexibility we will have,
- and how risk is shared between both parties.

# FC Porto / FA Manila Football Initiative Strategy

## Immediate strategy

Prioritize launching the Football World Camp before making any long-term commitment involving FA Manila or broader club operations.

## Purpose

Use the Football World Camp as the primary pilot initiative to validate operational, technical, and commercial collaboration between FC Porto and FA Manila before expanding into longer-term or broader club activities.

## Camp details

### Timing

- Target period: second week of school break in December.
- Scheduled during the school break to maximize youth availability.
- Planned before the next football season begins to serve as pre-season preparation and talent identification.

### Positioning

- Branded as a December Football World Camp delivered by FC Porto in partnership with FA Manila.
- Positioned as a premium, technically driven camp with direct FC Porto methodology and standards.

## Operational objectives

- Utilize FC Porto coaching and technical staff to deliver training content and methodology.
- Align camp sessions and activities with existing December football programs to create a coherent seasonal offering.
- Avoid scheduling conflicts with major tournaments, leagues, and school examinations to protect attendance.
- Maximize player participation across age groups by optimizing schedule, pricing, and communication.
- Maximize visibility for players, FC Porto, and FA Manila through structured evaluation, reporting, and promotion.

## Planning timeline and key milestones

Milestone

Key activities

Initial coordination launch

- Confirm intent to prioritize the Football World Camp.
- Define core roles and responsibilities between FC Porto and FA Manila.

Full operational planning complete

- Finalize camp structure, schedule, staffing, and participant capacity.
- Lock in facilities, equipment, and support services.
- Confirm communications, registration process, and safeguarding protocols.

Camp delivery window

- Run the Football World Camp during the targeted second week of the December school break.
- Capture technical, operational, and commercial learnings to inform any long-term engagement.
