PPA Asia / Hotel & Travel Partnership Coordination Update

Executive Summary

- Conducted coordination meeting with hotel and ecosystem partners on support structure for upcoming PPA Asia events, starting with the Crown Plaza event and potential expansion into Cebu and Davao.
- Partners underscored the need to justify media and commercial value, including international broadcast, livestream, and regional exposure benefits.
- Discussion expanded from hotel accommodations into a broader travel and lifestyle ecosystem across airline, hospitality, retail, and fintech partners.
- Robinsons Hotels & Resorts, Cebu Pacific, GoTyme, URC, Robinsons Retail, and Robinsons Hotels & Resorts were identified as key stakeholders for potential alignment.
- Positive initial alignment with strong interest in a long-term sports tourism and lifestyle platform anchored on the flagship Crown Plaza event and potential Cebu and Davao expansion.

Background / Objective

The meeting focused on coordinating hotel and travel partnerships to support upcoming PPA Asia events, beginning with the Crown Plaza event as the flagship and proof-of-concept. Cebu and Davao were explored as potential future tournament locations aligned with broader sports tourism initiatives.

Key Discussion Points

- Partners highlighted the need to clearly articulate media and commercial value, including:- International broadcast exposure
- Livestream reach and engagement
- Regional visibility and brand-building opportunities
- Scope expanded beyond hotel accommodations into a broader travel and lifestyle ecosystem that may include airline, hospitality, retail, and fintech stakeholders.
- Initial alignment was discussed for potential involvement from key partners:- Cebu Pacific (flight and travel support)
- GoTyme
- URC
- Robinsons Retail
- Robinsons Hotels & Resorts

Partner Landscape

- Hotel and hospitality:- Robinsons Hotels & Resorts (including Summit-branded properties and additional Cebu and Davao hotels)
- Airline and travel:- Cebu Pacific (discussed for potential flight and travel support)
- Retail and consumer goods:- URC
- Robinsons Retail
- Fintech:- GoTyme

Hotel Coordination

Cebu Properties

- Jenny Tuna from Robinsons Hotels & Resorts offered to prepare proposals and rate structures for Cebu hotels, including:- Summit Hotel Cebu
- Summit Galleria Cebu
- Additional Cebu properties under Robinsons Hotels & Resorts
- Team discussed creating a tiered accommodation structure for Cebu to cover distinct segments:- International pros and VIPs
- Sponsors and officials
- International amateur players
- General participants
- Potential Cebu hotel options discussed included:- Summit properties
- New Star
- Fili
- Dusit Mactan

Davao Properties

- Robinsons Hotels & Resorts indicated interest in including additional Davao properties in proposals.
- Potential Davao hotel options discussed included:- Dusit Davao
- Acacia and Gototans area options

Event Strategy

- The Crown Plaza event remains the primary flagship event and the immediate proof-of-concept for long-term partnership structures.
- Cebu and Davao are being positioned as potential expansion cities for future tournaments and broader sports tourism initiatives.
- Partners expressed strong interest in framing the initiative as a sports tourism and lifestyle platform rather than a single, standalone tournament.

Risks / Dependencies

- Partnership approvals depend on clearly quantified media value and exposure metrics to satisfy internal justification requirements for key partners.
- Hotel rate structures and room allocations are dependent on timely participant and rooming projections from the organizing team.
- Ecosystem participation from airline, retail, and fintech partners relies on coordinated commercial discussions and alignment on the long-term sports tourism platform vision.

Next Steps & Owners
