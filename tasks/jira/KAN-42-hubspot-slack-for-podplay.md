# KAN-42: HubSpot & Slack For PodPlay

## Description

HubSpot

A centralized CRM platform that helps KAVC and PodPlay manage leads, customers, sponsors, partnerships, and sales opportunities while improving business development and customer engagement.

Slack

A real-time communication and collaboration platform that helps KAVC and PodPlay teams stay connected, share information, coordinate projects, and improve operational efficiency.

Combined Benefit

Together, HubSpot and Slack provide a connected system for managing relationships, communication, and collaboration, enabling faster decision-making and more efficient operations.

## Comments

### aflores — 2026-06-24

The PodPlay x Kosmas SE Asia Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy is a structured, collaborative framework designed to launch and scale PodPlay’s sports venue technology across Southeast Asia in Q2 2026. The partnership is built on a clear division of labor: Kosmas owns the customer relationship within the territory, while PodPlay owns the core product and provides backend support.

The GTM strategy is organized into four operational streams to ensure deals move quickly without information loss across time zones

1. Sales and Pipeline Tracking
2. Sales Enablement
3. Contracting
4. Post-Sales Support & Onboarding
