# KAN-39: Podplay

## Description

PodPlay is an all-in-one platform designed to help sports venues manage their day-to-day operations more efficiently. It combines court bookings, memberships, tournaments, payments, scoreboards, video features, and venue management tools into a single system. The goal is to improve the player experience, simplify venue operations, and support the growth of sports facilities through technology.

### JC Golez — 2026-06-11

Hi Avvy. Thanks for that thought. 

So here I will guide everyone with the process of PodPlay once we are already securing a contract/partnership with a client. A good example is Active Play Taguig. 

Once a client moves forward with PodPlay:

- We will ask them some information for the creation of the SOW and MSA. Hopefully moving forward for faster TAT, our rates are aligned with Ryan or we can do it on our own because our pricing structure is different from the US. - For the SOW we need:- Corporate Name:Corporate Jurisdiction and Form (e.g. Delaware LLC):Corporate Address:Venue Name:Venue Address:Signatory Name:Signatory Title:
- Once the SOW and MSA are signed, we will introduce the client to our Customer Success Team in the states. - They will send over a few forms to the client to gather everything needed to configure their account and begin the design and build-out of their branded app.
- They will also schedule a kickoff call. This includes things like branding assets, facility information, membership structure, operating hours, access control requirements, and any other setup details specific to their business.
- At the same time, they will begin a series of training sessions with the client, so they start getting familiar with the platform while the app is being built. These sessions cover everything from reservations and memberships to payments, reporting, and day-to-day operations.
- As the app progresses, our team handles the configuration and setup work in the background. Once the app is ready, it we will review it together, test the entire user experience end-to-end, and make sure everything is working exactly as expected before launch.
- The goal is that by the time the app is live, your team is already trained, the platform is fully configured, and you're confident operating the system from day one.
- All these processes will take:- Around 3-4 weeks for the Basic+ feature
- Around 3 months for the Pro and Autonomous
