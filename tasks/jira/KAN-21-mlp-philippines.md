### Richard Bachmann — 2026-08-14

Here's a quick update on MLP:

1. MLP–KAVC Agreement
We're in the final stages of the contract between MLP and KAVC. There has been some back-and-forth on a few provisions, but we're making good progress toward finalizing it.
2. International Team
While the agreement is being finalized, we're moving ahead with forming our international team to compete in MLP.MLP is currently preparing the standard player agreement between MLP and each player. Since KAVC will be responsible for paying the players' salaries, we'll also require a supplementary agreement between KAVC and each player before Finance can release any funds. Our legal team is already drafting this.
3. Sponsorship
We'll need to begin engaging sponsors soon to support the team and league. I'd also like to get Trisha (Outlast) involved at this stage so we can start discussing sponsorship opportunities and commercial partnerships.
4. Domestic League Planning
MLP is giving us a two-year window to establish a domestic league in the Philippines, so we should start planning the roadmap early to ensure we're ready.
5. Potential MLP Asia Event in Manila
According to Kim, there's a possibility of hosting an MLP Asia event in Manila. One venue being considered is the Solaire Pickleball Court. It may be worth reaching out to the Solaire group or the Razon team to see if they'd be interested in hosting or partnering on the event.
